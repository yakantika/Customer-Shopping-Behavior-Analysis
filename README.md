# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior data to uncover purchasing trends, customer preferences, and key factors influencing buying decisions. The objective is to help businesses improve customer engagement, optimize marketing strategies, and increase overall revenue through data-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

A retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior across demographics, product categories, discounts, reviews, payment methods, and purchase frequencies.

The goal is to answer important business questions such as:

- Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
- Do subscribers spend more than non-subscribers?
- How do discounts influence purchasing behavior?
- Which products receive the highest ratings?
- What are the most popular products within each category?

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

- Handled missing values
- Removed duplicate records
- Standardized column names
- Created customer age groups
- Created purchase frequency features
- Converted categorical values into meaningful business attributes
- Prepared data for SQL analysis and reporting

## 🔍 Business Questions Answered

### Customer Analysis
- Total revenue generated by male vs female customers
- Customer segmentation into New, Returning, and Loyal customers
- Spending comparison between subscribers and non-subscribers

### Product Analysis
- Top-rated products based on review ratings
- Most purchased products within each category
- Products with the highest percentage of discounted purchases

### Sales Analysis
- Average purchase amount by shipping type
- Impact of discounts on purchasing behavior
- Revenue distribution across customer segments
